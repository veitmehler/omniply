Privacy Policy

Azavea Inc. · Effective November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Azavea Inc. ("Azavea", "we", "us") collects through this website, azavea.ai, and through the text messages we send, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you use the chat on this website, email us, call us, or ask to hear from us, we collect what you provide: your name, email address, mobile phone number, the name of your business, and the content of your message.

Your consent to text messages. When you agree to receive text messages from Azavea Inc., we keep a record of that consent: the mobile number, the date and time, and where you gave it.

Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic request data such as IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time. The chat on this site stores a small identifier in your browser so that a conversation can continue when you come back.

We do not buy personal information from data brokers, and this site runs no advertising trackers.

How we use information

We use the information above to answer your questions, to provide the services you ask for, to send you the text messages you agreed to receive, to keep our systems secure, and to meet our legal obligations. We do not use it for anything unrelated to why you gave it to us.

Text messages (SMS)

If you give us your mobile number and agree to receive texts, Azavea Inc. may text you about your inquiry, your account, appointments, and the service or system alerts you asked for. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help. The full SMS Terms are in our Terms & Conditions.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile numbers and SMS consent are shared only with the service providers that deliver our messages, such as our messaging platform and the mobile carriers, and only so the message can be delivered.

When we share information

We share personal information only with service providers that work on our behalf, such as our website host, our customer messaging platform and our email provider, under terms that limit them to that work. We may also disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect our rights and the safety of others. We do not sell personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiries and conversations for as long as we need them to respond and to maintain our business records. We keep records of SMS consent and opt-outs for as long as the law requires, so that an opt-out stays honored.

Your choices

You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP. You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by writing to team@azavea.ai. We will answer within a reasonable time.

Children

This website and our messages are meant for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

The Omniply product

Azavea Inc. operates the Omniply platform. Omniply has its own Privacy Policy covering the product and the data handled inside it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
