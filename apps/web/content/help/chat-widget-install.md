The chat assistant is a small bubble on your website that answers visitor questions about your practice, offers your guides, and captures callback requests straight into your Omniply CRM. It knows your hours, services, and policies, and it never gives medical advice.

## WordPress: automatic

If you connected your WordPress site during setup (with an administrator login), the widget installs itself: Omniply adds its small companion plugin, **Omniply Connect**, to your site and configures it. There is nothing to do. Open your website and look for the chat bubble in the corner.

The same plugin also quietly adds structured data about your practice (name, address, hours) to your site for search engines, another job you never have to think about.

## Any other website: one snippet

1. In your Omniply dashboard: **Settings → Chat Assistant**.
2. Click **Copy snippet**. It is a single line of code.
3. Paste it into your website's HTML just before the closing body tag, or hand it to whoever manages your site. Any platform that allows custom code works: Squarespace, Wix, Webflow, custom sites.

The bubble appears immediately after the change is live.

## What the assistant does

- Answers practical questions: hours, location, parking, services, pricing policy, what to expect at a first visit.
- Offers your lead-magnet guides and delivers them by email.
- Takes callback requests with name and number, which appear in your CRM with a conversation summary for your front desk.
- Refuses medical questions and directs emergencies to emergency services. It always tells visitors it is an AI assistant.

## Keeping its knowledge fresh

Everything it knows is editable in **Settings → Business Info and Chat Knowledge**: see [Editing your chat knowledge](/help/chat-knowledge). Changes reach the assistant within seconds.

## Taking over a conversation yourself

Every conversation the assistant has also appears in your CRM. If you want a human to take over with a specific visitor, add the tag **ai-off** to that contact and the assistant goes silent for them; your team can continue the conversation directly. The assistant also does this automatically when a visitor asks for a human.

## Common problems

- **No bubble on WordPress.** Check the site connection in Settings, or fall back to the snippet method; both work together.
- **The bubble shows but answers look outdated.** Update the knowledge in Settings; it refreshes within seconds.
