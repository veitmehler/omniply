The voice assistant answers phone calls in a clone of your own voice, any hour of the day. It takes the caller's name and number first (in case the line drops), answers practical questions with the same knowledge as your chat assistant, texts links on request, transfers to your team when they are in, and takes callback messages with the caller's preferred time when they are not. Every call lands in your CRM.

It is optional, and it is set up from the card on your dashboard or anytime under **Settings → Voice Assistant**.

## What you need

A voice account at **elevenlabs.io** (the voice technology provider). You create it yourself, on your own card, so your voice and your usage belong to you. The **Creator plan (about $22/month)** is what we recommend: it includes enough call minutes for most practices and full voice cloning. The Starter plan works for trying it out.

## The guided steps

1. **Create your ElevenLabs account** and open Profile → API Keys. Create a key and copy it.
2. **Paste the key** into the setup. Omniply verifies it on the spot.
3. **Your voice is cloned automatically** from the interview answers you spoke during onboarding. You get a preview to listen to. Not happy with it? You can re-record and re-clone anytime.
4. **Your assistant gets its own phone number.** Omniply provisions it for you; there is nothing to configure.
5. **Choose how you use it.**
   - **Missed-call backup** (most practices): keep advertising your normal number, and set your phone system to forward calls to the assistant's number when nobody answers, or outside opening hours. We show you the forwarding instructions.
   - **Direct line:** publish the assistant's number on your website and profiles so the AI answers first, every time.
6. **Enter your practice's real number** so "can I speak to a human?" transfers the live call to your team during opening hours. Outside hours the assistant takes a message instead of transferring to an empty office.

Make a test call when you finish. Ask about your hours, then ask for a human, and watch the results arrive in your CRM.

## Things worth knowing

- **Every call announces it is recorded** for quality assurance, and the assistant always says it is an AI. Both are required for compliance and built in.
- **If a transfer rings out**, the caller is brought back automatically and offered a callback instead of being stranded on hold.
- **Usage and cost** live in your ElevenLabs account; your dashboard shows a meter so quota surprises do not happen.
- Where leads and messages land: see [Leads and callbacks in your CRM](/help/leads-and-callbacks).

## Common problems

- **Key rejected.** Copy it again in full from ElevenLabs; keys start with "sk_".
- **The voice does not sound like you.** The clone quality follows your onboarding recordings. Re-record in a quiet room and re-clone from Settings.
