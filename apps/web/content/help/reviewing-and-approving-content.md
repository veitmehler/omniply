Everything Omniply writes waits for your approval. Articles, social posts, and newsletters all land in your review queue first, and only what you approve gets scheduled and published. This is deliberately your moment of control, and for most practices it takes about ten minutes a week.

## Where to review

Log in to your Omniply dashboard. Your **content plan** shows the coming days; anything waiting for you is marked for review. Open an item to read it in full.

## Reviewing an article

- Read it the way a patient would. The facts have already passed an automated fact-checker before reaching you, but you know your practice best.
- Want changes? Edit directly, or use the request-changes option to describe what to adjust and a revision comes back to the queue.
- Click **Approve** and the article is scheduled: it publishes to your website on its planned date, and its social posts are prepared around it.

## Reviewing social posts

Posts are shown as previews close to how they will look on each platform, including the video posts. Approve them the same way. A day's posts travel together, so approving is quick.

## Reviewing the newsletter

Your weekly newsletter arrives for review before its send day, with its articles inside. Approve it and the send is scheduled to your subscriber list.

## What approval triggers

- Articles publish to your site on their planned date, not instantly, so approving early is always safe.
- Social posts schedule to their planned day and times and publish automatically through your connected accounts.
- Everything you approved shows as scheduled in the plan, so you can always see what is coming.

## Good habits

- **Approve a few days ahead.** The system plans in advance precisely so a busy week at the clinic never leaves your feed empty.
- **Do not stockpile unapproved days and approve them all late.** Posts whose day already passed will not go back in time; keep the queue roughly current.
- Small voice tweaks you make in edits are noticed. The more you approve unchanged, the better the system has matched your voice.

## Common problems

- **Nothing is in my queue.** Right after onboarding, give the first generation run a few hours. Otherwise check the content plan for the next planned dates.
- **I approved something by mistake.** Open it in the plan and unschedule or edit it before its publish date.
