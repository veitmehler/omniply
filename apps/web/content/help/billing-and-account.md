## Your subscription

Omniply is one monthly subscription covering everything: the content engine, your Omniply CRM workspace, the funnels, the chat assistant, and the voice assistant infrastructure. Founding members keep their founders price for as long as their subscription stays active.

The only cost that lives outside Omniply is the optional voice assistant's voice account (ElevenLabs), which you hold directly on your own card so that your cloned voice always belongs to you. See [Setting up the AI voice receptionist](/help/voice-assistant-setup).

## Invoices and payment method

Your subscription is billed to the card you used at signup. Payment receipts arrive by email with each charge. To update your card or view past invoices, use the billing link in your welcome email, or contact support and we will send you a fresh secure link.

## If a payment fails

You will get an email, and the card is retried automatically. Content generation and publishing continue through a short grace period, so a expired card never blanks your feed overnight. If payment is not resolved after the grace period, publishing pauses until the subscription is current again; nothing is deleted.

## Pausing or cancelling

Contact support to pause or cancel; we handle it the same day.

- **Pausing** stops billing and new content generation. Your workspace, your contacts, and everything already published stay intact, and you can resume where you left off.
- **Cancelling** stops billing at the end of the current period. Your published articles remain on your website (they are yours), and your CRM data is retained for a period after cancellation so a return is painless, after which it is permanently deleted. If you want an export of your contacts before deletion, ask support and we provide one.

## Ownership, plainly

- Content Omniply wrote for you and you published is **yours**.
- Your contact list is **yours**; export anytime.
- Your cloned voice lives in **your** ElevenLabs account, not ours.

## Getting help

For anything billing-related, or anything at all, use the support contact from your welcome email. Founding members are onboarded personally, and that line stays open.
