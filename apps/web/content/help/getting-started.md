Omniply is a complete content marketing system for chiropractic practices. It writes and publishes your articles, social posts, and newsletters in your own voice, turns engagement into leads through automated funnels, and answers your website visitors and phone callers with an AI assistant that knows your practice.

## What you need before you start

- **Your website address.** Omniply reads it during setup to learn about your practice. If you have a blog you wrote yourself, even better: the system learns to write like you.
- **Access to your practice's Facebook page, Instagram account, and LinkedIn.** You will connect these once so posts can publish automatically.
- **About 15 minutes** for the onboarding conversation, plus a few minutes in your CRM afterwards.
- If your website runs on WordPress: an administrator login, so publishing and the chat widget can be set up for you.

## What happens when you sign up

The moment your purchase completes, Omniply builds your entire workspace automatically:

1. **Your Omniply CRM** is created, with pipelines, automations, and lead funnels already assembled.
2. **A welcome email** arrives with your login details.
3. **The onboarding conversation** is waiting for you on first login. It is a chat, not a form: it reads your website, asks you six questions you answer out loud, and lets you pick your call to action with one tap. See [The onboarding walkthrough](/help/onboarding-walkthrough).
4. As soon as onboarding finishes, **your first month of content starts generating**. Articles and posts appear in your review queue over the next hours.

## Your part, in order

1. Complete the onboarding conversation (about 15 minutes).
2. Do the three CRM steps: connect your social accounts, publish the two comment automations, and check your business profile. See [Connecting your social accounts](/help/connecting-social-accounts) and [Publishing your comment automations](/help/publishing-comment-workflows).
3. When content appears, review and approve it. See [Reviewing and approving content](/help/reviewing-and-approving-content).

From then on, your ongoing workload is about ten minutes a week in the review queue.

## Common questions

**Do I need any technical skills?** No. Everything is either automatic or a guided click-through.

**Does anything publish without my approval?** No. Every article and post waits in your review queue until you approve it.

**Where do I get help?** Every page of this help center, or the support contact in your welcome email.
