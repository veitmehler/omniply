Omniply plans your content thirty days ahead, so you can always see what is coming and approve at your own pace. Here is the rhythm.

## The weekly shape

- **Articles.** In-depth, patient-friendly articles publish to your website on their planned days. Every article is fact-checked automatically before it ever reaches your review queue.
- **Social posts, three per day.** A morning story post, a midday visual post, and an evening story post. The stories are two connected beats of one small narrative, written in your voice from your onboarding answers. On article days, the midday slot is a Key Takeaways video: a short looping video designed so viewers watch it repeatedly to read every point. On newsletter days, the midday slot is a designed carousel from that week's newsletter.
- **The newsletter, weekly.** A magazine-style email built from your articles, sent to your subscriber list after your approval.

Every post carries your call to action, usually the invitation to comment SPINE for the free Spine Check.

## Reading the plan

Your dashboard's content plan shows each day: what is planned, what is waiting for review, and what is approved and scheduled. Colors and labels distinguish articles, posts, and newsletters. Open any item to preview or approve it.

## Steering the topics

You can suggest article ideas anytime from the dashboard: type an idea or paste a link, and it enters the plan. The system otherwise plans topics for you, chosen for what chiropractic patients actually search for and worry about, and it will not repeat itself.

## What you do not need to do

- Pick posting times: the schedule is tuned for engagement.
- Write captions or hashtags: they are generated with each post.
- Balance platforms: each post is adapted per platform automatically, including how links and comment hooks are handled on Instagram versus Facebook and LinkedIn.

## Common problems

- **A day looks empty.** Check whether items for that day are still waiting for your approval.
- **I want fewer or different posts.** Talk to support; the cadence can be adjusted to your practice.
- **A topic does not fit my practice.** Skip it: edit or reject that item and the plan continues. Your suggestions always take priority.
