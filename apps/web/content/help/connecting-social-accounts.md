Your posts publish through the Social Planner inside your Omniply CRM. Connecting each platform is a one-time job of about five minutes.

## Where to go

In your Omniply CRM: **Marketing → Social Planner**. The first visit shows connect buttons for each platform; later you can manage connections from the settings (gear) icon inside the Social Planner.

## Facebook page

1. Click **Connect** next to Facebook and log in with the personal Facebook account that administers your practice page.
2. When asked which pages to allow, select your **practice page** and grant all requested permissions. Permissions you decline here are the number one cause of failed posts later.
3. Back in the Social Planner, make sure your practice page shows as connected.

## Instagram

Instagram connects through your Facebook login, and your Instagram account must be a **Business or Creator account linked to your Facebook page** (set that up in the Instagram app under Settings → Business tools if you have not already).

1. Click **Connect** next to Instagram.
2. Log in with the same Facebook account and select your Instagram business account.

## LinkedIn

1. Click **Connect** next to LinkedIn and log in.
2. Select your **company page** if you have one. You can also connect your personal profile; posts route to the company page by default.

## Check it worked

Each connected account appears with its avatar in the Social Planner. That is all Omniply needs; scheduled posts will publish to every connected account automatically once you approve them.

## Common problems

- **Instagram will not connect.** Almost always the account is still a personal account, or it is not linked to the Facebook page. Fix both in the Instagram app, then reconnect.
- **Posts fail with a permissions error.** Reconnect the platform and accept every permission on the Facebook screen. Facebook occasionally expires connections; reconnecting takes a minute.
- **Wrong page connected.** Disconnect it in the Social Planner settings and connect again, choosing the right page.
