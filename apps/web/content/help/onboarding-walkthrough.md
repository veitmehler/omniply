Onboarding is a chat, not a settings page. It collects everything Omniply needs to write in your voice and market your practice. You can pause anytime and pick up where you left off.

## Step by step

**1. Your website.** Paste your website address. Omniply reads your pages to learn your services, your location, and how you talk about your practice.

**2. Your blog, if you have one.** If the scan finds blog posts, you will be asked one important question: **did you write these yourself?** Only confirm this if the writing is genuinely yours (or your own team's). When you confirm, Omniply studies those posts as a writing sample so your new content matches your existing voice. If the posts were outsourced or AI-written, say so; the system will build your voice from the interview instead.

**3. Six spoken questions.** The heart of onboarding. You answer out loud, in your own words, using the microphone button. The questions cover who you are, who you help, what makes your practice different, and two or three true stories from running your practice: a mistake you fixed, a lesson that cost you something, a moment you are proud of. **Real moments only, and never patient details.** These answers are what your articles, posts, and story content get built from, so talk the way you would talk to a patient, not the way a brochure sounds.

**4. Your photos.** Upload a few real photos of you and your practice if prompted. Real beats stock, always.

**5. Your call to action.** Choose what every social post drives toward. Most practices pick the one-tap option: **Comment "SPINE" and we DM the free Spine Check**. This powers the automated lead funnel described in [The Spine Check funnel](/help/spine-check-funnel). You can also choose booking, newsletter signups, or a custom goal.

**6. Finish.** The finale screen kicks off your first generation run. Articles and posts start arriving in your review queue over the next hours, and your link-in-bio page and Spine Check page are published automatically.

## After the chat

Three quick steps remain in your Omniply CRM: connecting your social accounts, publishing the two comment automations, and checking your business profile. The next two articles walk you through them.

## Common problems

- **The website scan found nothing.** Check the address is exactly your live site (including https://). You can continue without a blog; the interview carries your voice on its own.
- **A voice answer went wrong.** Just answer again before moving on; the latest take counts.
- **You closed the tab mid-onboarding.** Log back in; the conversation resumes at your last step.
