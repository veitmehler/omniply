The Spine Check funnel is your practice's front door on social media. It works because it asks for the smallest possible action: one word in a comment.

## How it flows

1. **Every social post invites the same action:** comment the word **SPINE**.
2. **Your comment automations** (the two you published during setup) spot the comment and instantly send that person a DM with a link to your **2-Minute Spine Check**, a short self-assessment quiz on your own domain.
3. **They take the quiz** and enter their details to get their results. That makes them a contact in your CRM, tagged and identifiable.
4. **Follow-up runs automatically.** They receive their results, and your guide sequence begins: over the following days they get your lead-magnet guides by email, each one building trust and pointing back to booking.
5. **Your job** is what it always was: when they book or ask for a call, your front desk takes it from there. See [Leads and callbacks](/help/leads-and-callbacks).

## Why one word works

A comment costs the visitor nothing and no context switch. The DM arrives while your post is still on their screen. And every comment also boosts the post's reach, so the funnel feeds the content and the content feeds the funnel.

## The parts that must stay exactly as they are

The funnel is wired end to end, and renaming any link in the chain breaks it silently:

- **The keyword is SPINE.** Every generated post says it; the automations listen for it. Do not change one side without the other, and preferably change neither.
- **The trigger links** inside your automations (the quiz link and the guide links) are tracked links the system points at your pages automatically. Do not rename, delete, or replace them.
- **The quiz page and guide delivery** are managed by Omniply. Editing the page design is fine through Omniply; deleting the page is not.

If you want a different keyword or asset someday, contact support and we change it consistently across posts, automations, and links.

## Checking the funnel is healthy

Once a month, comment SPINE on one of your own posts from a personal account. You should get the DM within a minute, and the quiz should load. If either fails, check the two automations are still published (see [Publishing your comment automations](/help/publishing-comment-workflows)), then contact support.

## Common problems

- **DM never arrives:** an automation is unpublished, has the wrong page selected, or the comment misspelled SPINE.
- **Quiz link loads an error:** contact support; do not attempt to relink it yourself.
- **Leads arrive but no follow-up emails:** check the contact got tagged; if tags are missing, the comment may have come in through a different path. Support can trace it.
