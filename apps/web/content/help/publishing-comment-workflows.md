Two ready-made automations came with your workspace. They watch your Facebook and Instagram posts, and when someone comments the word **SPINE**, they automatically send that person a direct message with your free Spine Check. This is the engine of your lead funnel.

They arrive **unpublished on purpose**: each one needs to know which Facebook page and which Instagram account is yours, and only you can select that. It is two minutes of clicking.

## Before you start

Your social accounts must already be connected. If you have not done that yet, do [Connecting your social accounts](/help/connecting-social-accounts) first.

## Facebook comment automation

1. In your Omniply CRM: **Automation → Workflows**.
2. Open the workflow whose name mentions **Facebook comment reply**.
3. Open the trigger step (the first block). In its settings, select **your Facebook page** from the dropdown, then save the step.
4. Top right, switch the workflow from Draft to **Publish**.

## Instagram comment automation

1. Back in **Automation → Workflows**, open the workflow whose name mentions **Instagram comment reply**.
2. In the trigger step, select **your Instagram account**, save.
3. **Publish** it the same way.

## Test it (recommended)

Once your first posts are live, comment **SPINE** on one of them from a friend's account or your personal profile. Within a minute a DM should arrive with the Spine Check link. That is the whole funnel proving itself.

## What not to change

- **Do not change the keyword.** Every post's call to action says SPINE; the automation listens for SPINE. If either side changes, the funnel silently breaks.
- **Do not rename or delete the links inside the automation steps.** They are wired to your quiz and guides.
- Adding your own extra steps (for example an internal notification) is fine, as long as the existing steps stay intact.

## Common problems

- **The page dropdown is empty.** The social account is not connected yet, or the connection lost permissions. Reconnect in Marketing → Social Planner, then reopen the workflow.
- **Comments get no DM.** Check the workflow shows Published (not Draft), the right page is selected in the trigger, and the comment actually contains the word SPINE.
- **It works on Facebook but not Instagram** (or vice versa). Each platform has its own workflow; check the other one is also published with the right account selected.
