title: Editing your chat knowledge
description: "Control everything your AI assistants know: questions and answers, hours, and free-form practice notes."

---

Your chat assistant and your voice assistant share one brain, and you control what it knows. All of it lives in **Settings → Business Info and Chat Knowledge** in your Omniply dashboard. When you save, the assistants pick up the changes within seconds; there is nothing to redeploy or restart.

## What you can edit

**Practice phone and booking page.** The number the assistant gives out and the booking link it shares.

**Opening hours.** Your hours are normally read from your Google listing automatically, so the assistant can answer "are you open right now?" correctly, including on the phone. If your Google hours are wrong or you want different wording, the hours you enter here override them.

**Questions and answers.** A list of question and answer pairs, seeded during onboarding. These are the assistant's most reliable knowledge: parking, insurance policy, what to bring, cancellation policy, whether you treat children. Add a pair whenever you notice visitors asking something the assistant deflected. Keep answers short and factual, the way your front desk would say them.

**Anything else the assistant should know.** A free-form box (up to 5,000 characters) for practical facts that do not fit the question format: "Free parking behind the building." "We accept HSA and FSA cards." "New patients should arrive ten minutes early." "Dr. Smith is out Fridays." The assistant treats this as reference information and weaves it in naturally when relevant.

## What you cannot make it do

The assistant will not give medical advice, diagnose, promise treatment outcomes, or discuss individual patients, no matter what you put in the knowledge. Those guardrails are fixed, and they protect your practice. Emergencies are always directed to emergency services.

## Good habits

- **After your first week, read a few transcripts** (in your dashboard) and add Q&A pairs for anything it had to deflect to the front desk. Ten minutes of this makes the assistant noticeably sharper.
- **Keep it current.** Holiday hours, a new service, a practitioner on leave: one line in the free-form box handles each.
- **Never paste patient information here.** The knowledge is for facts about the practice, not about people.

## Common problems

- **The assistant gives an outdated answer.** Find where that fact lives (Q&A pair, hours, or the free-form box), correct it, save. Ask the widget again a few seconds later to confirm.
- **The assistant says "the front desk can help with that" too often.** That is its honest fallback for things it does not know. Add the missing facts and the deflections disappear.
