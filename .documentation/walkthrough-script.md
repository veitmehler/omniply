# Omniply Walkthrough — video script v1

Target: 4–5 minutes main cut. One scene = one recording take (15–45s of
screen activity). Narration is written for Veit's voice (ElevenLabs clone or
self-read), ~140 words/min. Record each scene's screen activity to roughly
match the narration length; assembly can stretch b-roll, never speech.

Production notes
- Account on screen: simonchiro (post-E2E) or the demo account. Browser
  100% zoom, notifications off, clean tabs.
- The ONLY date in the whole video is in Scene 10 (easy re-render).
- The CRM's native onboarding wizard is OFF by design (never name the underlying vendor anywhere on screen or in narration: the white-label name is Omniply); Scene 3 replaces it.
- Numbers spoken in narration are written out the way they should be said.
- Per-scene clips are saved individually; scenes 3 (CRM setup), 4
  (onboarding), 7, 8, 9 double as tutorial embeds for /help articles.
- ORDER FIX 2026-09-10: CRM setup (scene 3) comes BEFORE the Omniply chat
  onboarding (scene 4) — matches reality; email/text services get ONE
  honest sentence here, full detail lives in a help article + later
  tutorial video (user decision).

## Scene 1 — Cold open: the problem (~25s)

ON SCREEN: Quick montage, 3–4s each: a finished article on a clinic site,
a Key-Takeaways video post looping on an IG feed, a story post, a CRM
contact record with a callback note. No UI chrome yet, just the outputs.

NARRATION:
"If you run a chiropractic practice, you already know the problem. The
marketing that actually grows a clinic, articles, social posts, newsletters,
follow-up, is a part-time job nobody in the building has time to do. So it
gets done badly, or it gets done never. Omniply is how it gets done anyway.
Watch what your first month looks like."

## Scene 2 — What you get + purchase (~20s)

ON SCREEN: The pricing section on the website, cursor over the plan, click
through to the checkout, order form filled, purchase confirmation.
(One-shot clip: capture during the E2E, or a mocked order form.)

NARRATION:
"One plan, everything included. When you sign up, the system builds your
entire workspace automatically: your CRM, your pipelines, your automations,
your lead funnels. By the time you open your welcome email, the machine is
already assembled. Your part starts in your new CRM, and it's quick."

## Scene 3 — Your CRM setup first (~50s)

ON SCREEN: Inside the Omniply CRM (the white-labeled sub-account). Step
captions on screen. [TERMINOLOGY: Veit fills the exact menu names during
script editing.] In order: (a) Business details and sender info: name,
address, timezone, website. (b) A 2-second glimpse of the email and text
service screens (no detail). (c) Marketing → Social Planner → connect
Facebook page, Instagram account, LinkedIn. (d) Automation → Workflows →
open the Facebook comment workflow, pick the connected page, Publish; same
for Instagram.

NARRATION:
"First stop is your new CRM, and we walk you through every step. Check
your business details and sender info, so everything that goes out carries
your name. You'll also connect your email and text services here, with a
guide that walks you through each screen, and while their approvals run in
the background, you keep going. Connect your social accounts, Facebook,
Instagram, LinkedIn. Then publish the two ready-made automations that turn
comments on your posts into leads in your inbox. Open each one, choose
your page, hit publish. Done. Now the fun part."

## Scene 4 — Onboarding chat (~45s)

ON SCREEN: The onboarding chat embed. Show, in order: pasting the website
URL and the scan finding blog posts, the "I wrote these myself" confirmation
tap; one voice question being answered by speaking (mic UI visible); the
call-to-action step with the one-tap 'Comment "SPINE" → we DM the free Spine
Check' option; the finale screen kicking off generation.

NARRATION:
"This chat is the actual setup, about fifteen minutes, once. It reads your
website, and if you have a blog, it asks one important question: did you
write it? Because if you did, it learns to write like you, not like an AI.
Then it asks you six questions, and you answer them out loud, in your own
words. Your story, your patients, what makes your practice different.
That's what your content gets built from. Pick your call to action, and
you're done. From here, Omniply starts writing your first month."

## Scene 5 — The content engine (~40s)

ON SCREEN: The Omniply dashboard. Scroll the 30-day content plan; open a
generated article; then the social side: a Key-Takeaways music video post
playing, a story post carousel, a newsletter preview.

NARRATION:
"Here's what it built. A thirty-day plan: articles your patients actually
want to read, social posts every day, and a weekly newsletter. And these
aren't generic clinic posts. The videos are made to be watched on loop, the
stories are written in your voice from the answers you spoke, and every
claim has to survive a fact-checker before you ever see it."

## Scene 6 — Review and approve (~25s)

ON SCREEN: The review queue. Open a pending article, skim, click Approve;
show a social post preview and approve it; the status flipping to
scheduled.

NARRATION:
"Nothing goes out without you. Everything lands in a review queue first,
read it, tweak it if you want, approve it. One click and it's scheduled.
Most clinics spend ten minutes a week here. That's the entire ongoing
workload."

## Scene 7 — The lead funnel (~35s)

ON SCREEN: An IG post with the SPINE call-to-action; a comment "SPINE"
being posted; the automatic DM arriving with the Spine Check link; the
quiz on a phone frame; the new contact appearing in the Omniply CRM with tags.

NARRATION:
"Now the part that pays for everything. Every post invites one small
action: comment the word SPINE. When someone does, they instantly get a
message with your two-minute Spine Check. They take it, and they're in
your CRM, tagged, followed up, nurtured automatically. Content isn't the
product. Patients are."

## Scene 8 — AI chat on your website (~30s)

ON SCREEN: The clinic website with the chat bubble; a short visitor
conversation asking about hours and booking; the visitor leaving a
callback number; cut to the Omniply CRM: the contact + note + front-desk notification.

NARRATION:
"Your website gets a receptionist too. The chat assistant knows your
practice, your hours, your services, and it never gives medical advice.
It answers questions, takes callback requests, and drops every lead
straight into your CRM with a summary your front desk can act on. It
installs itself on WordPress. You don't touch a line of code."

## Scene 9 — The AI voice receptionist (~40s)

ON SCREEN: A phone dials the clinic's AI number. Real call audio (record
an actual call): the greeting with the recording line and "Who am I
speaking with?", the caller giving a name, asking a question, requesting
a callback for a time; cut to the Omniply CRM: contact, note with preferred time,
notification SMS.

NARRATION:
"And for the calls you can't pick up, this. A receptionist that answers
in your own cloned voice, any hour. It takes their name and number first,
in case the line drops. It answers real questions, and when they want a
human, it takes a message with the time they want to be called, and your
team sees it instantly. Listen, that's not me, that's the AI."

## Scene 10 — Close (~20s)

ON SCREEN: Back to the marketing page. The founders offer block, the
date, the button.

NARRATION:
"Articles, social, newsletter, funnels, chat, and a voice receptionist,
running from one fifteen-minute setup. Doors open September twenty-second.
The founding cohort gets onboarded personally and keeps the founders
price for life. The link is right below this video. I'll see you inside."
