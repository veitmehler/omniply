## Scene 1 — Cold open: the problem

ON SCREEN: Quick montage, 3–4s each: a finished article on a clinic site,
an IG slide carousel mid-swipe, a Key-Takeaways video post looping, a
newsletter on a phone, a CRM contact record with a callback note. Outputs
only, no UI chrome.

NARRATION:
"When the world looks like this, will your practice survive the next
three years?

Now, if you run a chiropractic practice, the next three years will change
how patients find you. It's starting right now already.

Because your patients don't scroll through search results anymore. AI
hands them the summary answer at the very top of the search results. And
soon, they won't even search for a chiro anymore. Their AI assistant will
do it for them.

So what does that really mean?

AI agents will read your website, check your reputation, and book the
appointment for new clients all automatically. If the agents can't find
your site or don't understand it, and your site can't answer the agent,
then you're not on the short list. The agents will simply book the
appointments with another practice down the road that can talk to the
agents.

The AI assistant wars have already started between all the major AI labs.
So the time to optimize for this is now. It's essential. Because clinics
that were late to the Internet or to social media lost big during that
time. And this transformation will be even bigger and hit local practices
first.

And that's on top of the problem you already know. The marketing that
actually grows a clinic, like Google reviews, articles, social media
posts, newsletters, follow-ups, that's a part-time job in itself that
nobody in the building has time to do, right? So it gets rushed or it
never gets done.

But the even bigger problem now is that you have to continue publishing
content on your website even though hardly anyone will find it because AI
just summarizes the answer. You can't even measure the impact of your
content anymore.

So the real question becomes, can an AI agent even find your website
right now? Is your site set up for GEO, or how they call it, generative
engine optimization, that AI can actually scan it and read it? Likely
not.

Because the biggest problem is this: an AI assistant most likely won't
understand what your practice is all about when it visits your website
because AIs don't read your website like a human does. And could an AI
assistant ask your site a question and get a proper answer right now?

If not, I have good news for you. That's exactly why we built Omniply.

Because Omniply will handle all of this for you, costing you just a
couple of hours a month with coffee in hand. All you have to do is check
the content, approve it, and your whole month of marketing is taken care
of. Optimized for humans and AIs.

No extra staff needed, no developer needed, and no social media manager,
no designer, nothing else.

So let me show you how this works..."
