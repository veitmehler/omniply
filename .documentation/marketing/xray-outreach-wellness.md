**Status: v1 — 2026-10-30** (wellness-sales-funnel Phase 6; mirrors the chiro
v2 AI-first structure)

Low-volume, human outreach to US wellness & longevity center owners. ONE job:
get them to run the X-Ray at **omniply.io/x-ray?v=wellness**. The moment they
engage, outreach ends and the funnel (quiz → report → nurture) takes over.

## Target list — who and where

- **Categories** (Google Maps/Places, Yelp, Instagram location pages):
  wellness center, recovery lounge/studio, HBOT/hyperbaric clinic, IV
  therapy lounge, cryotherapy studio, cold plunge & sauna studio, longevity
  clinic, stretch/bodywork studio with memberships. Independent operators
  first — franchise corporate (Restore, iCRYO, Pause) buys differently.
- **Geography:** US metros with dense boutique-wellness scenes (Austin,
  Miami, Scottsdale, San Diego, LA, Denver, Nashville, Tampa) + tourist/
  resort markets where the five-language receptionist is a visible edge
  (Hawai'i, ski towns, beach towns).
- **Qualifiers worth 30 seconds before adding to the list:** has a booking
  link, has under ~100 Google reviews (the review pitch lands hardest), owner
  identifiable on LinkedIn/IG.
- Personalization slots: {{first_name}}, {{center}} (only when known — never
  guess), optional {{city}}.

## Rules of engagement (same as chiro)

- **LinkedIn/IG DM primary.** Hand-sent, 15–20/day max, no automation tools.
  Personalize the first line where it's effortless; never fake familiarity.
- **Cold email secondary**, never from the product domain: dedicated
  lookalike domain, own mailbox, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, ~2 weeks warm-up, 10–20/day.
  Plain text — cold email should look typed, because it is.
- **Handoff rule:** any reply or quiz completion → stop the sequence; replies
  get a human answer from Veit, not a template.
- **Exit with grace:** three touches, then done.
